description: Track natural disasters and climate patterns (El Niño/La Niña, floods, droughts, hurricanes, heatwaves, wildfires, earthquakes) that could affect operations, supply chains or crops of owned companies and real-estate projects.
---
Sources: NOAA CPC ENSO outlook, NOAA NHC, Copernicus/EDO drought, EM-DAT/ReliefWeb, USGS, national met services of the home country. Match events/forecasts to `regions[]`/`hazards[]`/`chokepoints[]` in the exposure map and to collateral locations of alt-invest projects (flood zones). Output: event, location, expected severity/timing, affected positions + weight, historical analogue (e.g. prior El Niño effect on cocoa/coffee/copper/shipping), confidence. Seasonal hazard calendar (hurricane season Jun-Nov, monsoon, northern-hemisphere wildfire/flood season) included in weekly report.
