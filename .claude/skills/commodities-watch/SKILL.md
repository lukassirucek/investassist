---
name: commodities-watch
description: Monitor commodity prices and supply news (precious and industrial metals, energy, agriculture, uranium) and map moves to owned positions - gold holdings, miners, producers and companies with a material input cost. Use weekly, and daily for held or key-input commodities.
---
Scope = `commodities` in `config/watchlist.yaml` + commodities held (asset_class `gold`/`commodity`, crypto excluded) + `raw_materials` of holdings in `data/exposure/holdings_enriched.csv` (table `exposure_raw_material_touching.csv`, overlapping, indicative). For each commodity: spot level and 1d/5d/30d/YTD change with source and date (WebSearch/WebFetch; open the page, never use a search snippet), then the drivers (supply cuts, inventories, OPEC+/producer decisions, weather and harvest, sanctions, export bans, central-bank buying, real yields and the dollar for gold, demand data from China/US). Flag a move >= 10% in 5d for an input of an owned company (threshold in `config/alerts.yaml`) and any physical/logistics disruption (chokepoints: Hormuz, Red Sea, Black Sea, Malacca).

Output per commodity: level and change, driver (FACT with URL + date), affected positions with value in base currency and % of portfolio (join `raw:<material>` tags to the exposure map), scenario note (INFERENCE, a range not a forecast), dated catalysts in the next 14 days (inventory reports, OPEC+ meetings, expiries). Log material items with `scripts/signals.py` using tags `raw:<material>`. Cross-check with `seasonality` for the current window (HISTORICAL PATTERN) and with `geopolitics-sanctions` when a driver is political. Price history is reference data for the report, not a signal by itself: no move, no signal.
